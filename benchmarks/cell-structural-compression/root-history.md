The previous training cursor only started at roots recognized by the installed
switch. That biases future Bake candidates toward the current catalog. Successful
uncompiled producers now retain bounded ancestry in diagnostic training mode:
PC remains zero, unit is null, and no executable result history is invented.
Ordinary execution without training retains its existing behavior. History v2
reports uncompiled roots and dropped events; v1 remains accepted.

History admission considers bounded suffixes as well as full recorded paths.
Their supports overlap and must not be summed as saved applications. A missing
producer root and its joins are proposed atomically within the unit budget.

authorize admission. Producers with no extra result reuse are rejected. New
producer leaves require rebasing portable join IDs; every old path is checked
against the reparsed catalog. Runtime representations remain existing Cell slots,
query entries and completion programs.

## Next optimization boundary

A history identifies a candidate transition, not proof that a new instance is
saturated. Bake must establish the inputs, completed effects, reusable result
sources and original publication boundaries. Runtime checks keep required
visibility, lifetime, mutable-read and union obligations live. Only then may a
compiled transition omit established RHS operations and run its remaining suffix.
Matching, new RHS effects and uncovered repair remain separate costs.

Collect a sufficiently sized bounded trace before ranking global opportunities.
Measure eliminated constructor/key/binding/publication operations against added
checks, retained slots and dispatch; do not award credit for a longer history or
for merely reaching a compiled state. No new sum-bits closure-admission runtime
benefit or peak-memory reduction is claimed in this change.
